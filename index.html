About

I'm a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Virginia Tech, advised by Prof. Yan Chen. My research asks how people and AI can collaborate in real time — designing interfaces where AI assistance stays legible, steerable, and genuinely useful in the middle of live work.

During my M.S. at UC San Diego, I worked with Prof. Haijun Xia on natural language interfaces that let people converse with their own data. I earned my B.Eng. in Computer Engineering from Chulalongkorn University, where I was supervised by Prof. Boonserm Kijsirikul on brain–computer interfaces — my first taste of designing for a signal messier than any user ever is.

I've also shipped production interfaces as a software engineer at Amazon Alexa, on the Alexa Comms Calling Experience team, and at Refinitiv (Thomson Reuters), building DTaaS, a cloud platform for inter-bank FX deal tracking.
